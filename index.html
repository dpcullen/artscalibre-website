Why Families Choose Us

School should feel right for your child.

Maybe the traditional classroom isn’t working. Maybe your child learns differently, moves at their own pace, or simply needs a place where they’re seen as an individual — not a number. Maybe you’re homeschooling and want community without the rigid system. Or maybe kindergarten is around the corner and you’re not sure the conventional route is the answer.

You’re not alone. And there is another way.

ArtsCalibre Academy is an independent, arts-enriched school in Victoria, BC serving students from Kindergarten through Grade 9. We pair small class sizes with a creative, hands-on curriculum that meets every learner exactly where they are.

Our Approach

Education without limits.

We don’t believe in one-size-fits-all. Our arts-integrated, student-centred model gives every child the freedom to learn in the way that works for them.

Individualized Learning

No two students learn the same way. Our teachers use flexible, multi-age pods and differentiated instruction to meet each child at their level — not the system’s level.

Arts as a Way of Learning

Visual arts, music, and drama aren’t add-ons — they’re woven into every subject. Art is how our students process, express, and deeply understand the world around them.

Safe, Inclusive Community

We foster a warm, respectful environment where every child — regardless of how they learn or who they are — feels safe enough to take risks, ask questions, and be themselves.

The Whole Child

Academics matter — but so do confidence, empathy, and emotional resilience. Through programs like MindUP and The Leader in Me, we nurture the whole person, not just the student.

“We encourage students to approach their studies with unbridled curiosity — to think unconventionally and question the norm.”

— ArtsCalibre Academy

What They’ll Learn

A full curriculum, creatively delivered.

Language Arts

Reading, writing, and speaking brought to life through storytelling, poetry, music, and imaginative play.

Mathematics

Numeracy developed through rhythm, cooking, building, and hands-on problem solving.

Science

Inquiry-driven exploration enhanced by creative projects, experiments, and real-world connections.

Social Studies

History, geography, and culture explored through literature, theatre, music, and visual arts.

Visual Arts

Drawing, painting, sculpture, and mixed media as both creative expression and a tool for learning.

Music & Drama

Performance, composition, and dramatic arts build confidence, teamwork, and emotional expression.

Physical Education

Movement through sports, yoga, dance, and outdoor education — body and mind together.

French

Language and cultural understanding through conversation, songs, and creative art forms.

Admissions

Your journey starts here.

Getting to know your family is the first step. Our five-step process is designed to make sure ArtsCalibre is the right fit — for your child and for our community.

1

Complete the Discovery Form

Tell us about your child and what you’re looking for. This short online form helps us understand your family.

2

Attend an Information Session

Join us for a virtual or in-person session to learn more about our philosophy, programs, and community.

3

Visit & Shadow

Tour our campus and let your child experience a day in our school. See firsthand what makes ArtsCalibre different.

4

Receive Your Assessment

We’ll share our recommendation: an offer of admission, a spot on our waitlist, or an honest conversation about fit.

5

Welcome to the Family

Accept your offer, complete registration, and begin your ArtsCalibre journey. We can’t wait to meet you.
